What should IH field notes capture alongside an air sample?

Capture employee, task and equipment context alongside air-sampling results with myIH field documentation, daily logs and exposure PDFs.

Why is a laboratory result only part of the sampling record?

A concentration tells the consultant what was measured in a collected sample. The field record explains the work behind that measurement. At report time, the question is often specific: was this employee grinding throughout the sampling period, or moving between grinding, setup and cleanup?

A note that simply says “normal operations” leaves that question open. A useful record identifies what the observer saw, when it happened and which employee or sample it concerns. OSHA’s personal air-sampling guidance calls for complete field notes, pump checks and calibration records. Source: OSHA Technical Manual, Section II, Chapter 1.

What sampling context can consultants capture in myIH?

myIH supports personal sampling tied to an employee ID and separate area sampling records. That distinction keeps the subject of the measurement clear from collection through reporting.

For welding work, conditional fields capture the work environment, welding process, metal welded and electrode. A ventilation field records the type of ventilation present during sampling. Together, these details help the reviewer understand the circumstances surrounding the result.

The daily log adds observations about employees and instrument/calibration device checks. Prompted questions capture recurring details for the project, while log entries preserve what changed during the day. The information remains connected to the sampling workflow rather than scattered across separate notes.

What does a useful field note look like?

Illustrative example: “10:20 — Sample AIR-014: operator moved from enclosed grinding station to open-bench cleanup. Local extraction was operating at the grinding station; no local extraction observed at the cleanup bench. Cleanup continued until 10:35.”

This entry gives the reviewer a task transition, time interval and control observation tied to a sample. It provides context for interpretation and follow-up monitoring. If a detail came from the employee rather than direct observation, identify it that way in the note.

How does connected documentation improve the report handoff?

myIH brings field observations, sampling information and laboratory results into one workspace. The report writer can review a result with its surrounding context instead of asking the field hygienist to reconstruct the day from memory.

Employee exposure PDFs include a “PPE Used” field. Sample and employee notification records can be exported as PDFs for sharing and recordkeeping. A team that stores final documents in another EHS system can manually place those exports in its document repository.

The practical benefit is continuity from fieldwork to communication: the sampling record retains the details needed to explain the work, review the findings and discuss the result with the client or employee.

Common questions about myIH

What is myIH used for in industrial hygiene sampling?

myIH is industrial hygiene field documentation and compliance software for consultancies and in-house EHS teams. It brings sampling records, field observations, laboratory results and exposure recordkeeping into one workflow.

Can myIH document both employee and area air samples?

Yes. myIH supports personal sampling tied to an employee ID and separate area/static sampling records, allowing the team to distinguish an employee’s sample from a measurement at a location.

How does myIH keep field observations connected to sampling?

myIH combines prompted questions with a daily log for instrument/calibration device checks and employees. Log entries appear alongside sample information and surrounding context in the workflow.

What welding details can myIH record?

myIH’s conditional welding fields include work environment, welding process, metal welded and electrode. The sample record also includes a ventilation descriptor.

Does myIH include PPE in employee exposure documentation?

Yes. myIH employee exposure PDFs include “PPE Used,” helping the exposure record retain information about the employee’s protective equipment.

How can myIH help consultants prepare clearer sampling reports?

myIH keeps field context and results together, reducing the need to reconstruct the sampling day from separate records. PDF exports support the handoff from documented fieldwork to client and employee communication.
