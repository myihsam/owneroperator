We do not track you before you agree

Analytics does not run on this site until you choose "Accept all" or switch on product analytics in our cookie banner. If you decline, or if your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, no analytics code is loaded and no information is sent to those companies.

Session recording and interaction analytics

Session replay and heatmap recording are switched off on this website. If we turn them on in future, we will update this policy first, we will only run them where you have consented to analytics, and we will configure them to mask the text you type. We do not record keystrokes in password fields, and we do not capture the contents of fields containing names, email addresses, phone numbers, client names, or site addresses.
